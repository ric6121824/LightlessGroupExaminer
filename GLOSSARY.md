# Lightless Group Examiner

The examiner compares the selected character's LightlessSync syncshell memberships and shows which players are shared between groups.

## Language

**Diagnostics snapshot**

A record of the selected character's LightlessSync state captured when a diagnostics report was generated.

**Online player**

A player whom LightlessSync reported as online in the diagnostics snapshot.

**Unique player**

A player who belongs to exactly one of the selected character's syncshells in the analyzed data.

**Unique online player**

A unique player who was online in the diagnostics snapshot.
